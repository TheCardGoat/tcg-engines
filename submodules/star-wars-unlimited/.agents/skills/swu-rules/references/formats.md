# Formats

Use for deckbuilding, constructed legality, sealed, and draft.

## Citations

- `9.1`: Two-player constructed general rules.
- `9.2`: Premier format.
- `10.1`: Limited general rules.
- `10.2`: Sealed format.
- `10.3`: Draft format.

## Implementation Notes

- Leaders and bases count for deckbuilding but are not considered part of the
  deck during gameplay.
- Aspect mismatch is generally allowed in deck construction; playing the card
  may incur aspect penalty.
- Keep format legality separate from runtime command legality.
