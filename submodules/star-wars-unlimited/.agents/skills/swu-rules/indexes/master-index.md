## Common Lookup Routes

- Keywords such as Ambush, Bounty, Exploit, Grit, Overwhelm, Piloting, Raid,
  Restore, Saboteur, Sentinel, Shielded, and Smuggle: start with
  `references/glossary.md`, then `references/abilities-and-effects.md`.
- Timing questions about attacks, playing cards, or action abilities: load
  `references/action-timing.md`.
- Targeting, choosing, search, prevent, modifiers, uniqueness, or control
  changes: load `references/additional-rules.md`.
- Zone movement, capture, resources, hidden information, or set-aside cards:
  load `references/zones.md` and then `references/additional-rules.md`.
- Deckbuilding, leaders, bases, and aspect legality: load `references/formats.md`
  plus `references/game-concepts.md`.
