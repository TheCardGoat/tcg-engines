# Functional Errata (Errata Bulletin #2)

This historical bulletin remains linked by the official errata index, but its
legacy host is no longer resolvable from the local downloader. This local
preservation records all of its rule-bearing topics for progressive discovery;
use the source URL when exact historical typography or image-based text is
needed.

## Seeds of Agony

- May be played from its owner's banished zone.
- The next qualifying attack action card gains a separate "When you attack"
  arcane-damage trigger for each Seeds of Agony effect applied to it.
- Therefore, if two effects are applied to the same attack, they generate two
  distinct 1-arcane-damage triggered effects; prevention applies separately to
  each event.

## Vestige of Sol

- If a card was put into the hero's soul that turn, pitching a Light card is a
  replacement-effect event: gain that many resource points plus one.
- It is not a delayed trigger that waits to resolve after pitching.

## Become the Arknight

- The bulletin records a non-functional erratum that clarifies the card's
  non-modal wording when its discard option is unavailable or declined.
