# Errata Bulletin #1

The following cards have received functional errata.

## Ira, Crimson Haze

Ira, Crimson Haze was originally printed in 2018 as part of the face card of the Welcome Deck that shipped to stores around to world to launch Flesh and Blood on October 11, 2019.

When we printed Ira, we knew at some point in the future we wanted a fun and fast format variant to exist, but the details of what that looked like were yet to be decided. That format ended up being Blitz, which features young heroes.

After Blitz was announced, it was apparent that fans wanted Ira to be included. With the release of Crucible of War, Ira, Crimson Haze gains the Young sub-type, making her officially legal to play in Blitz.

### Errata

Young sub-type added

## Gambler’s Gloves

We’re taking a reroll on how this card is worded.

The intention is very obvious. You use Gambler’s Gloves to reroll Scabskin Leathers, Bonehead Barrier, Crazy Brew, Kayo hero effect…. Unfortunately, how this card was printed as a triggered ability doesn’t achieve this intention.

### Errata

If an effect would resolve that includes rolling a 6 sided die, instead you may destroy Gambler’s Glove. If you do, the controller of the effect rerolls all 6 sided dice rolled this way. (You decide whether to use Gambler’s Gloves after seeing the result of the die roll.)
