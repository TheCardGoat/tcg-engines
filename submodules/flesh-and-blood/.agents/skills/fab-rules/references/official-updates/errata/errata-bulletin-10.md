# Errata Bulletin #10

Errata are effective Monday, 9 March, 2026.

## Compendium Cards

#### Cheating Scoundrel

Cheating Scoundrel’s printed wording implies that its wager effect is a continuous effect that can be applied to a card when it’s played. However, wager is a discrete effect that needs to happen after the attack resolves and the defending hero is established. This update restructures the wager as a triggered effect that occurs when the attack actually attacks, matching established wager templating.

The next attack action card you play this turn gets +3 and “When this attacks, wager with the defending hero. The winner creates a Gold token.”

#### Snarky prick

Snarky Prick’s original wording unintentionally caused the destruction of the top card of the defending hero’s deck to be optional or mandatory depending on whether that color of that card was verifiable by the opponent. To remove this discrepancy and simplify resolution, this update makes the destruction optional in all cases, ensuring the card behaves consistently regardless of the information available to the opponent.

When this attacks a hero, look at the top card of their deck. If it’s red, you may destroy it. If you do, this gets +4.

## Old Cards

#### Specifying weapon attacks

Back in Rosetta, with the Comprehensive Rules 2.9 update, we formally separated weapons from the attacks they generate to ensure that effects modifying a weapon attack do not also modify the weapon itself. Several existing cards (Helm of Sharp Eye, Merciless Battleaxe, and Zephyr Needle) were written prior to this distinction and continued to reference a weapon’s power rather than the power of the weapon attack, creating ambiguity under the updated rules. This update to their text ensures the cards reference the weapon attack’s power, rather than the weapon’s power, so that they will work as intended.

#### Current funnel

Current Funnel unintentionally retained a non-triggered resolution ability during the Rosetta proofing process. This update adds a “when this attacks” trigger, aligning the card with current templating and providing a clear point of timing for its effect.

When this attacks, if the last action card you played this turn was Lightning, this and the next action card you play this turn get go again.

#### earthlore bounty

Earthlore Bounty’s original wording was ambiguous between triggering multiple times and creating a single token each trigger, or triggering once and creating multiple tokens. This update is to officially confirm that when an effect draws you multiple cards, Earthlore Bounty’s triggered effect will trigger once and create that many tokens.

Whenever you draw 1 or more cards from an action card effect, create that many Seismic Surge tokens.

#### murky water

Murky Water was originally proofed under the assumption that trap cards would only exist as deck-cards – that no longer holds with the introduction of trap equipment. Because Murky Water banishes traps face-down and selects one at random, this may result in unverifiable or illegal outcomes where arena-cards (equipment) may end up in the deck. This update restricts Murky Water to only being able to banish cards that can be played (i.e. ensuring that only deck-cards end up in the arsenal zone).

When this hits, you may banish 3 traps with cost 0 or more from your graveyard face-down. If you do, choose one at random and put it into your arsenal.

#### northern winds

Northern Wind is a troublesome card on two fronts. It lacks a definitive timeframe for its freeze effect and it uses the “and/or” function in conjunction with “each hero”. The former was addressed with an update to the rules, but the latter still does not convey the correct way to play the card.

We’ve decided to update the rules AND this card moving forward. Freeze will be specified by a timeframe on effects moving forward, and Northern Winds will have true text that correctly communicates how the card is played.

Oldhim Specialization

For each hero, freeze up to 1 equipment, item, and ally they control until the start of your next turn.

Unity – When this defends together with a card from hand, create a Spellbane Aegis token under any number of heroes’ control.

#### pummel

This errata formalizes a functional update introduced with Pummel’s Welcome to Rathe – Unlimited reprint. The update clarifies that the trigger requires the attack to hit a hero for them to discard a card, and does not reference the defending hero. This ensures that hitting an ally (or similar) will not trigger the effect and cause the defending hero to discard a card.

Target attack action card with cost 2 or more gets +4 and “When this hits a hero, they discard a card.”

#### Shifting Tides

Shifting Tides’ original text assumed the card would always be controlled by its owner, which can lead to incorrect interaction if it were played by another hero. This update ensures that the card goes to the correct player’s deck if the condition is met.

At the start of your turn, pitch the top card of your deck. If it’s blue, put this on the bottom of its owner’s deck, otherwise destroy this.

#### thaw

The third mode of Thaw’s effect was originally intended to be able to unfreeze cards in arsenal, however, with targeting being limited to objects in the arena or on the stack, an update is needed to ensure that it works as intended.

While this is in your graveyard, at the start of your turn, you may banish it and choose 1:

- Destroy target Frostbite.
- Destroy target Ice affliction.
- Unfreeze target frozen card in the arena or an arsenal.
