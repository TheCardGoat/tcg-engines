## Product Configuration

### Expansion Slot

The High Seas Expansion Slot features 14 powerful cards covering a range of heroes and classes outside of those featured in High Seas.

The Expansion Slot replaces a basic rarity card (formerly known as token rarity) in around 1 in 15 boosters.

Expansion Slot cards are removed from the pack in limited formats and kept by the person who opened them, just like Cold Foils and Legendary-rarity cards.

To do this, remove the last two cards from the booster pack before adding the rest to your card pool. These two cards will be one of the following combinations:

- 2 basic cards
- 1 basic card + 1 Expansion Slot card
- 1 basic card + 1 Legendary-rarity card
- 1 basic card + 1 Marvel-rarity card

Non-basic cards removed this way cannot be included in a Limited deck.

### Treasure Pack Configuration

- 43 different Lost Treasures to discover (in addition to the 90 card set)
- 17 unique Lost Treasure (only 1 of each exist in Treasure Packs)
- Lost Treasure appears ~1:1000 Treasure Packs
- 1 Treasure Pack per display\*
- 1 Gold Slot
- 1 Map Slot
- Treasure Slot contains a Cold Foil or a Lost Treasure

\*Each High Seas booster display contains 1 Treasure Pack. They are not available for individual sale.
