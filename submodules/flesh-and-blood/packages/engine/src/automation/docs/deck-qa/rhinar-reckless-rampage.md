## Core interaction analysis

- Rhinar intimidates whenever a 6+ power card is discarded during his action phase, regardless of whether the discard is random; effects that specifically require a random 6+ discard (Beaten Trackers) are narrower. Every intimidate owns only its face-down card and returns it at end turn unless another effect moves it.
- Ravenous Meataxe draws then randomly discards on attack, gets +2 on 6+, and thereby triggers Rhinar. Monstrous Veil and Sand Sketched Plan are additional random-discard routes. Gauntlets require 6+ in pitch; Savage Sash discounts 6+ attacks; Scabskin supplies seeded RNG action points.
- Defensive core includes Reckless Swing's random-discard cost/damage, Scowling Flesh Bag intimidate, Crown cycling, Thick Hide Hunter's defend discard, and clash cards. Defense-time discard is not in Rhinar's action phase on the opponent turn, so it must not trigger Rhinar.
