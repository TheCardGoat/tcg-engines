## Core interaction analysis

- Both Oscilio versions share `Once per Turn Instant — discard an instant: draw a card` and Essence of Lightning. The official Rosetta note confirms a split card with an instant side can pay this discard cost. The once-per-turn limit is shared across every priority window in the turn.
- Discarding an instant turns on Starfall and Volzar, Meteor Storm's prerequisite, but it does **not** count as playing a Lightning card for Volzar, the Lightning Rod. The Lightning Rod's X is the Lightning cards played before its ability resolves, then that Amp affects exactly the next nonzero arcane-damage event. A Lightning Instant may be played on top of the activation to raise X; a later Lightning card cannot change the locked value. Constella Uplift can untap a staff only when its own conditions permit it. The Silver Age loadout uses Twinkle Toes/Voltic Vanguard after an instant; the CC loadout adds Cap of Quick Thinking, Lightning Greaves, Shock Charmers, and Stonewall Gauntlet.
- Defense spans proactive shields (Cloud Cover), reaction defense/opt (Fate Foreseen), equipment prevention, and Stonewall's combat-chain-wide power reduction. Instant cards may be played in combat, but ordinary actions cannot unless another effect permits it (CR 7.0.1a).
