## Core interaction analysis

- Zyggy's instant costs 2, taps Zyggy, destroys a controlled Lightning Flow, and banishes another Lightning aura permanent with no holo counters. That aura returns as a new arena object with one holo counter. Cosmic Duality supplies the Flow; Fleeing Starbreeze or Nourishing Glow supplies the other Lightning aura.
- Iris of Reality turns Illusionist auras into 4-power weapons during the action phase. Shimmers grows an aura weapon once each turn. Dream Weavers removes and prevents phantasm only from the next Illusionist attack action, not aura-weapon attacks.
- Defense covers Mirage (Flicker Trick), Ward on Lightning auras, ordinary Sink/Shelter, and Crown cycling. Fragment is a defender-power trigger distinct from phantasm: each card with defense 2+ makes the attack lose 2; phantasm checks a non-Illusionist 6+ power attack action defender.
