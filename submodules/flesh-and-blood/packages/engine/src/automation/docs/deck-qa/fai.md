## Core interaction analysis

- Fai may start with Phoenix Flame in graveyard. His once-per-turn instant costs 3 minus the number of Draconic chain links controlled; the cost bottoms out at zero but still requires a legal Flame in graveyard. Each attack must become a real chain link before it counts.
- Searing Emberblade gains go again with two Draconic chain links. Pouncing Paws creates a playable Crouching Tiger in banished; Tearing Shuko buffs only the next Tiger. Kunai is destroyed when combat closes after its attack. These are separate chain-lifecycle assertions.
- This list has few conventional defensive cards. Blade Beckoner Helm and Bloodied Oval provide dynamic defense; Mask/Arcane Lantern provide arcane prevention; Arcane Polarity rewards having actually been dealt arcane damage rather than merely being targeted.
