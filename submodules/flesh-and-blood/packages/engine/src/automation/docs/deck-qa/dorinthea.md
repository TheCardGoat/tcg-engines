## Core interaction analysis

- Once each turn, when Dorinthea's weapon hits, the weapon may attack one additional time that turn. This is permission, not an action point or go again; the line still needs a legal continuation and activation payment.
- Dawnblade's second hit adds a permanent +1 power counter; at end phase all counters are removed if Dawnblade did not hit that turn. Glistening Steelblade can add counters on each hit. Refraction Bolters grants go again after a hit, and Grains may turn a paid hit trigger into Vigor.
- Reprise reads whether the defending hero defended from hand when the reaction resolves. Kabuto's defend trigger blocks later enemy weapon attacks. Crown of Providence, Sink Below, Shelter, and equipment prevention exercise defensive movement/replacement paths.
