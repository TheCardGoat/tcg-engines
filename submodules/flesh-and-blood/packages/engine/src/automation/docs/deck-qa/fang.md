## Core interaction analysis

- Fang creates one Fealty whenever Fang's controller hits an already marked hero. The fixture can exercise token creation, but its only weapon is Decimator Great Axe, not a dagger; therefore the `3 or more Fealty` dagger-cost clause is not reachable from this deck list and needs a separate legal hero-contract fixture rather than fabricated dagger legality.
- Felling Swing/Cleave modify the next axe attack; Decimator halves the base defense of the first non-equipment defender. Braveforge is legal only after a weapon hit. Oath of Loyalty must be the first action and restricts all later played cards that turn to Draconic.
- Pillar of Unity checks a same-event equipment-plus-hand defense. Kabuto prevents later opposing weapon attacks that turn. Steelblade Shunt/Blunten punish weapon attacks, while Fatal Engagement, Overpower, and Provoke test the attacker reaction side.
