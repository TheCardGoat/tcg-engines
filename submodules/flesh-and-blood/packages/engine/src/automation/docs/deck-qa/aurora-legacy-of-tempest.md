## Core interaction analysis

- Aurora's instant pays 2, taps her, destroys a Lightning Flow, and creates Embodiment of Lightning. Voltbound Duality creates the Flow from hand; Flowstate Embodiment can create Flow or Embodiment when an instant is played on its chain link.
- Scorpio attacks only while Aurora controls a Lightning attack and deals one arcane on hit. Aether Ironweave requires both an attack action and a non-attack action played that turn. Grasp creates Runechant with a cost that scales per current Runechant. Snapdragon grants go again only in an attack-reaction window to cost-1-or-less attacks.
- Face Purgatory is the core defense interaction: it needs equipment, attack action, and non-attack action defending together. The list has no defense reaction; its defensive proof comes from block values, Face, equipment durability, and instant interaction during the opponent's combat.
