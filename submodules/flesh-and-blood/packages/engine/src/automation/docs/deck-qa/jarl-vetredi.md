## Core interaction analysis

- Jarl triggers for every Ice card played and creates a Frostbite in one opponent equipment zone that is exposed—empty under CR 3.0.1a. Occupied zones are never candidates; with no exposed zone, creation fails without inventing a fifth zone.
- Gauntlets of the Boreal Domain have independent Earth-pitch (+2 power to Mangle) and Ice-pitch (dominate) branches. Heart of Ice taxes opposing cards and activated abilities for the turn. Stalagmite creates Frostbite for the attacking hero when it defends; Rampart can be paid each time it defends.
- Rootbound Carapace/decompose, Staunch Response's additional payment, Pulse of Isenloft's turn-wide defense modifier, Blizzard's current erratum, and ordinary Sink Below are distinct defense/payment/replacement boundaries.
