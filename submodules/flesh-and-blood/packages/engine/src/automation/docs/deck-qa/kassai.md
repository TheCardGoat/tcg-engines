## Core interaction analysis

- Drawing any card that turn reduces Kassai's sword activation costs by one for the rest of that turn. Her once-per-turn action banishes exactly two red and two yellow graveyard cards; only the next weapon hit creates one Gold. The cost is graveyard movement, not hand consumption.
- Cintari Saber gains +1 when defended by attack actions. Hot Streak gets go again after being defended by an attack action. Grains creates Vigor after weapon hits and a paid resource; Valiant Dynamo removes one -1 defense counter at end phase after two weapon attacks.
- Crown of Dominion creates starting Gold; Raise an Army spends Gold, while Blood on Her Hands spends Copper—tokens are not interchangeable. Defense core includes Crown of Providence, Kabuto, Blunten, That All You Got?, Fate Foreseen, Sink Below, and Shelter.
