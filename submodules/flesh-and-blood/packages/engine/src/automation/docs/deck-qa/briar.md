## Core interaction analysis

- Errata Bulletin #4 is the oracle: the first attack action Briar controls that deals damage to an opposing hero creates Embodiment of Earth; playing the second non-attack action each turn creates Embodiment of Lightning. Neither trigger is simply “first attack played” or “second action of any kind.”
- Star Fall gets +1 and go again after a Lightning card is played. Blitz Kicks requires an instant card played that turn and directly creates Embodiment of Lightning. Swiftstrike Bracers require Nimblism. The Blade Beckoner set and Bloodied Oval are defensive loadout choices with dynamic defense.
- Sigil of Suffering is a defense reaction that deals arcane damage, then checks whether arcane damage was dealt for its defense bonus. Split cards/meld, instant-side timing, and Lightning Flow conditions need real priority windows.
