## Core interaction analysis

- Every opt event puts energy counters on Blaze equal to cards looked at. Blaze can remove exactly X once per turn to banish a Wizard non-attack action from hand whose effect deals X arcane damage, then grants permission to play it that turn as an instant. Counter amount, printed effect amount, class/type, and zone must all match.
- Talismanic Lens (opt 2), Seeker's Mitts (prevent 1 then opt 1), and spell-based opt are the hero enablers. Crucible increases the next arcane effect by one; it must not change which card was eligible for Blaze's X-cost check. Mage Master Boots grants a non-attack action go again, not instant timing.
- Defensive core: Absorb in Aether is a defense reaction that buffs the next arcane card in the same turn; Dampen both deals and shields; Unflinching Foothold removes dominate; Unyielding Grip checks empty hand; Spellfire Cloak is opponent-turn-only resource plus Arcane Barrier.
