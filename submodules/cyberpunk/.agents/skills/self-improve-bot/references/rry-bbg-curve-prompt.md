# RRY and BBG curve prompt

Use this when coaching or playing `authored-rry-llorona-steel-dragon` against
`authored-bbg-towerfall-control`. Spendable €$ is ready Eddie-area cards plus
Legends that can pay 1 €$ each (face-down, or face-up with a Sell Tag). Legends
are not Eddies. A sold card enters the Eddie area ready. Spent Eddies and spent
Legends ready at the start of your next turn, except the first player's two
left-most Legends, which stay spent through turn 1 (CR 7.7.4). One main-phase
sell per turn.

After that sell, and assuming a sell every turn after, the ladder is the same
shape from either seat: turn 1 is 2 €$ on the play and 4 €$ on the draw, then
5, 6, 7, and 8.

## Turn 1 resources

On the play, two Legends are spent and cannot pay. One ready Legend pays 1 €$,
and the sell adds 1 ready Eddie: 2 €$ total. Only the right-most Legend can be
Called. Do not spend both the Call and Trust No One.

On the draw, all three Legends pay and the sell adds 1 ready Eddie: 4 €$. Call
any of the three. That 4 €$ pays a Call, Jacked-In, and Trust No One together,
or a Call plus All is Lost or The Heist.

| Your turn | Spendable €$ after the sell | On the play | On the draw |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 on the play, 4 on the draw | Call the one ready Legend. | Choose the Call. The extra 2 €$ pays the turn-1 package. |
| 2 | 5 | The other Legends now pay. | Same 5 €$. |
| 3 | 6 | 3-drop, or the program that lets Jacked-In attack. | Same. |
| 4 | 7 | Meredith, 6th Street, or Pepe. | Same. |
| 5 | 8 | Equip the blades, or play Lizzy. | Same. |

## RRY — Steel Dragon on curve

Sell a spare program: All is Lost, Industrial Assembly, The Heist, Bonnie and
Clyde, Over the Edge, or Carnage at the Colosseum.

- On the play, turn 1 (2 €$): sell, then Call the one ready Legend.
- On the draw, turn 1 (4 €$): sell, Call Muamar Reyes or Dexter DeShawn: Off the Grid (not V: Streetkid), and still play All is Lost or The Heist.
- Turn 2 (5 €$): All is Lost if no curve unit is in hand, or The Heist when a Gig shows 1 or 2 and no blade is in hand.
- Turn 3 (6 €$): La Llorona or Dexter DeShawn: One Last Chance.
- Turn 4 (7 €$): Meredith Stout or 6th Street Recruits.
- Turn 5 (8 €$): Mantis Blades, Zetatech Faceplate, or Satori on those bodies. Do not equip a Legend and do not Go Solo V.

Later, Yorinobu Arasaka may play a unit of cost 4 or less from hand or trash
for free, and that unit can attack rival units this turn. In this list that is
Meredith, 6th Street, La Llorona, or Dexter DeShawn: One Last Chance.

Mantis Blades (+2 power) and Satori (+2 power, draw when the host wins a fight)
belong on 6th Street, Meredith, Trauma Team, or Yorinobu. Zetatech Faceplate
belongs on Meredith or La Llorona. When that host is spent, adjust a Gig by up
to 1, then draw if you control 3 or more Gigs with different values.

La Llorona is a Blocker and increases a Gig by up to 3 when she blocks.
Meredith is a Blocker with +2 power while fighting a Legend. When a rival
adjusts or swaps one or more of your Gigs, you may add a card from your trash
to your hand. Dexter DeShawn: One Last Chance adjusts a Gig by up to 1 on play
and on attack. Trauma Team costs 6, reduced by 1 for each unit in your trash,
minimum 1.

V: Streetkid, on Call, trashes 3 and adds a Braindance from that trash to your
hand. The Braindances here are Industrial Assembly, Bonnie and Clyde, and
Carnage at the Colosseum. Dexter DeShawn: Off the Grid, on Call, gives a
friendly unit +2 power this turn or draws 1. His Spend increases a Gig by up
to 2. Muamar Reyes, on Call, stops a friendly unit from being defeated in a
fight this turn, or draws 1. His Spend adjusts a Gig by 1.

All is Lost trashes 3 and adds a unit from among them to your hand. The Heist
trashes 4 and adds a Gear. If that Gear's cost equals a friendly Gig's value,
you may play it for free. Mantis Blades cost 1. Satori and Faceplate cost 2.
Industrial Assembly increases a Gig by up to 4 and draws if you control a Gig
at 8 or more.

Bonnie and Clyde defeats a rival unit of power 4 or less, or two if the rival
controls at least 2 more Gigs than you. Carnage defeats a rival unit with less
power than a friendly unit. Over the Edge defeats a unit whose power is at
most your d20's value.

## BBG — Towerfall control

Sell a spare program: Floor It, Three Mouths, One Desire, Nocturne OP55 N1,
Pyramid Song, Les Élémens, or Towerfall. Keep Chrome Reverie, Trust No One,
and Peace Offering for Lizzy and the Gig lines.

- On the play, turn 1 (2 €$): sell, then Trust No One if a friendly Gig shows 2–4. Otherwise Call the one ready Legend. Not both.
- On the draw, turn 1 (4 €$), with Jacked-In and Trust No One in hand: sell, Call a Legend, play Jacked-In, and set that 2–4 Gig to a min with Trust No One. If either card is missing, use the on-the-play line.
- Turn 2 (5 €$): Jacked-In if he is not already out. He does not attack this turn.
- Turn 3 (6 €$): Peace Offering if you lack a value-pair, otherwise Trust No One, otherwise Floor It. Then attack with Jacked-In.
- Turn 4 (7 €$): Pepe Najarro. Attack only when a value-pair would ready Alt Cunningham and Jackie Welles. Hanako Arasaka is not Merc.
- Turn 5 (8 €$): Lizzy Wizzy, if a program from the table below is in your hand or trash. The free play is one of those programs.

Lizzy plays any of these for free, then bottom-decks it:

| Program | Cost | Play it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chrome Reverie | 3 | A rival unit cannot attack until your next turn. With a min Gig, you may Call a Legend for free. |
| Nocturne OP55 N1 | 3 | Choose one: draw 2, a unit cannot attack until your next turn, or a friendly Legend may Go Solo for 2 €$ less (minimum 1 €$). |
| Pyramid Song | 3 | A rival unit gets −4 power. If a friendly d4 is a min Gig, also bottom-deck a rival unit at power 0. Otherwise choose one. |
| Three Mouths, One Desire | 2 | Look at the top 3. Keep 1, plus 1 more for each friendly min Gig. |
| Trust No One | 1 | Decrease a Gig by up to 3. Draw if you then control a min Gig. |
| Peace Offering | 1 | You may copy one Gig's value onto another. Draw if you then control a value-pair. |
| Floor It | 1 | A rival unit gets −1 power this turn. Draw 1. Quick. |

Jackie Welles, on the first blue unit or blue gear you play each turn, may
decrease a friendly Gig by up to 2 and draws if it becomes a min Gig. That
min Gig is what lets Chrome Call for free, Pyramid Song take both modes, and
Three Mouths keep the extra card. Peace Offering makes the value-pair Hanako
draws from at the start of your turn. Pepe's attack readies up to 2 Merc
Legends when you have one.

Placide, on play or attack, may discard 1 program to bottom-deck a rival unit.
Maman Brigitte may discard 2 programs to bottom-deck an unequipped rival unit.
Alt Cunningham spends 1 €$ and herself to replay a program from trash, still
paying its cost, then bottom-decks it. Towerfall gives all rival units −5
power and bottom-decks rival units at power 0. If you have less Street Cred
than the rival, it does both. Les Élémens bottom-decks the rival's
lowest-power unit. Psycho Squad is a 4-cost, 6-power unit with no ability.
