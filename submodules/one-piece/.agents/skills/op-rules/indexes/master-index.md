Version: One Piece Card Game Comprehensive Rules 1.2.0, last updated January 16, 2026.

## How To Use

1. Load `../references/glossary.md` first.
2. Pick the smallest matching section below.
3. Read that numbered section in `../comprehensive-rules.md`.
4. Cite rule numbers in the final ruling or implementation handoff.

- 1. Game Overview: players, defeat, concession, card-text precedence, impossible actions, simultaneous choices, numeric rules.
- 2. Card Information: card names, categories, colors, types, attributes, power, cost, card text, Life, Counter, Trigger, card number.
- 3. Game Areas: deck, DON!! deck, hand, trash, Leader area, Character area, Stage area, cost area, Life area, open and secret areas.
- 4. Basic Game Terminology: effects, players, turn player, active/rested states, draw, damage processing, playing cards, "up to", "if/then", "remove", set power to 0.
- 5. Game Setup: Leader, 50-card deck, 10-card DON!! deck, color legality, mulligan, starting Life.
- 6. Game Progression: Refresh, Draw, DON!!, Main, and End phases; playing cards, activating effects, giving DON!!, battle action.
- 7. Card Attacks and Battles: attack declaration, target legality, Block Step, Counter Step, Damage Step, end of battle.
- 8. Activating and Resolving Effects: effects, validity, activation costs and conditions, resolution, trigger ordering, pending effects.
- 9. Rule Processing: defeat judgment and rule-processing timing.
- 10. Keyword Effects and Keywords: [Rush], [Double Attack], [Banish], [Blocker], [Trigger], [Rush: Character], [Unblockable], [Main], [Counter], DON!! xX, DON!! -X, [On Play], [When Attacking], [On Block].
- 11. Other: infinite loops, revealing cards, viewing secret areas.
