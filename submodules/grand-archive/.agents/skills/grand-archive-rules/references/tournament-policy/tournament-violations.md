# Tournament Violations

Please refer to the [Infractions Policy Guide](https://www.gatcg.com/article/infractions-and-penalties-guide), our document explaining general philosophies, ideas, and guidelines for resolving infractions in a tournament setting. As described in the document, deviations from the document in resolving infractions are only allowed during extraordinary circumstances. This document will be regularly reviewed and updated so tournament attendees should ensure that they stay up to date with the most current version of the document. The potential penalties given can range from a warning to a complete disqualification from a tournament, based on the violation and its severity regarding tournament integrity and fairness. Disqualifications must be be accompanied by eviction of the disqualified player from the event venue.

The following pages will cover the following topics that have significant impacts on tournament operations beyond the that state of any game or match played.

* [Cheating](/tournament-violations/cheating.md)
* [Unsporting Conduct](/tournament-violations/unsporting-conduct.md)
* [Bribes](/tournament-violations/bribes.md)
* [Betting](/tournament-violations/betting.md)
* [Timeliness](/tournament-violations/timeliness.md)
* [Outside Assistance](/tournament-violations/outside-assistance.md)
* [Team Violations](/tournament-violations/team-violations.md)

Please consult the Infractions Policy Guide for additional insight and recommendations on general rules for handling infractions. The Infractions Policy Guide will have specific information on philosophies, definitions, and methods to correct the common infractions in a tournament, in addition to the special sections listed above.
