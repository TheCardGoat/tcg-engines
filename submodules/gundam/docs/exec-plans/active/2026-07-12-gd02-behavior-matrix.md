executable definition and its public-behavior test, records the printed timing
family, names the main positive/negative qualification boundary, and flags the

protocol. Concern tags are review axes, not permission to assert internal shape.

where GD02 has no qualifying printed support: Zechs, OZ, White Base Team,
Riddhe, Operation Meteor, Maganac Corps, Machu, Sayla, Mikazuki, Akihiro,
Suletta, Coordinator, Elan, and Academy.
